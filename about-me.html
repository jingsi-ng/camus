About This Project

This website was created as Assignment 3 for the Web Authoring module. It showcases the life and work of Albert Camus, a personal hero whose philosophy of absurdism has been a source of comfort and inspiration.

The design philosophy reflects Camus's own aesthetic: minimalist, honest, and focused on what matters. The sand color palette is inspired by his Algerian homeland.
