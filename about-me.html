I am a student at Griffith College Dublin, studying Interactive Digital Media. I am interested in technology, design, and how we tell stories online. This website is my tribute to Albert Camus, a writer and thinker who has changed how I see the world.

About This Website

When I started working on this project, I wanted to do something more than just showing information. I wanted to create something that feels like reading Camus, not just reading about him.

That is why every design choice here has a meaning. The sharp edges show Camus's direct and honest style. The golden sand colour comes from Algeria, where he grew up. The animations on each book page, like the rolling boulder and the hot sun, are not just decoration. They represent ideas from his philosophy.

Building this site taught me something important: good design is like good philosophy. You have to remove everything that is not necessary, until only what matters is left. Camus called this "the revolt", which means choosing to create meaning through what we do. This website is my small revolt.
